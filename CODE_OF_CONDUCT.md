# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Welcome!

Thank you for being part of this project.

Our goal is to build a friendly, respectful, and inclusive community where everyone can learn, collaborate, and contribute.

---

## Our Standards

Please:

- 🤝 Be respectful to everyone.
- 💬 Communicate kindly and professionally.
- 🎓 Share knowledge and help others learn.
- 🧠 Be open to constructive feedback.
- ❤️ Respect different opinions and experiences.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Offensive language
- Personal attacks
- Spam or disruptive behavior
- Any form of intimidation

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## Thank You

Together, we can create a welcoming learning environment for everyone.

Happy coding! 🚀
